The offering

A Greg Skog residence on 2.79 undivided acres above the Pacific.

Set on 2.79 undivided acres in upper Launiupoko, 70 Iliahi Way is a single-level residence by Maui architect Greg Skog, sited on a protected hillside for ocean, mountain and outer-island views. Walls of folding glass open the great room to more than 700 sq. ft. of covered lanai and a heated infinity-edge pool set on the line of Lānaʻi across the channel.

The residence

Indoor and outdoor living arranged around the view.

The kitchen, with marble countertops and premium appliances, opens to the great room. The house has two bedrooms, three baths and no interior stairs. A breezeway connects it to the two-car garage structure, which includes approximately 385 sq. ft. of flex space with a full bath, not included in the living area, along with laundry.

The land

2.79 acres, never divided.

The parcel is 121,416 sq. ft. of agricultural land in the Mahanalua Nui subdivision, sited so the residence and pool face the channel while the hillside above remains open. It has never been CPR'd, which leaves that option, and the choice of a larger main residence subject to County requirements, to a future owner.

Aerial boundary lines are approximate. Development is subject to Maui County rules and approvals; buyers should verify zoning, permits and development rights independently.

Location

Upper Launiupoko, West Maui.

Launiupoko is a neighborhood of view acreage on the slopes south of Lahaina, between the West Maui Mountains and the channel to Lānaʻi. Homes sit on large agricultural parcels reached by private lanes, and the beach park at the foot of the hill is a few minutes away.
